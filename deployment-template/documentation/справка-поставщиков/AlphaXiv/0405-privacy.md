# Privacy Policy

**Last revised:** September 24, 2026

This privacy notice for alphaXiv ("alphaXiv," "we," "us," or "our") explains how we collect, use, disclose, retain, and delete information when you use our website, mobile applications, AI assistant features, and related services (collectively, the "Services").

If you have questions or privacy requests, contact us at <a href="/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#fe8e8c97889f9d87be9f928e969f869788d0918c99" class="text-custom-red hover:underline"><span class="__cf_email__" data-cfemail="0e7e7c67786f6d774e6f627e666f76677820617c69">[email protected]</span></a>.

## Delete Your Account

You can delete your alphaXiv account in any of these ways:

- **In the mobile app**, open Settings, then Account, select "Delete account", and confirm.
- **On the web**, open <a href="https://www.alphaxiv.org/settings" class="text-custom-red hover:underline" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">alphaxiv.org/settings</a>, go to the Danger section, select "Delete account", and confirm.
- **Without signing in**, email <a href="/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#7606041f0017150f36171a061e170e1f0058190411" class="text-custom-red hover:underline"><span class="__cf_email__" data-cfemail="c4b4b6adb2a5a7bd84a5a8b4aca5bcadb2eaabb6a3">[email protected]</span></a> from the address on your account and ask us to delete it. We may ask you to confirm the request before we delete the account.

You do not need to cancel a paid subscription first. Deleting your account ends any paid subscription immediately, and the current billing period is not refunded.

Deletion takes effect immediately and cannot be undone. It deletes:

- your profile, profile photo, email addresses, sign-in methods, passkeys, sessions, and API keys;
- your assistant chats, records of your assistant requests, and the files you uploaded to the assistant;
- your comments, votes, folders, bookmarks, follows, custom feeds, notifications, and settings;
- your private papers and your paper submissions;
- papers and blog posts you published on alphaXiv, which are removed from the site along with their comments;
- images you uploaded to comments;
- your analytics profile.

We keep the following after deletion:

- **Billing records** held by our payment provider, Polar, as required for tax and accounting.
- **Analytics events and usage records**, which are no longer linked to your account profile.
- **Public researcher profiles** built from publicly available information such as paper authorship, even if you claimed one. Email <a href="/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#3545475c4354564c755459455d544d5c431b5a4752" class="text-custom-red hover:underline"><span class="__cf_email__" data-cfemail="88f8fae1fee9ebf1c8e9e4f8e0e9f0e1fea6e7faef">[email protected]</span></a> to request changes to or removal of a researcher profile.
- **Images you added to shared wiki pages.**
- **Backup copies**, until they are replaced in the normal backup cycle.

See <a href="#6-data-retention-and-deletion" class="text-custom-red hover:underline">Data Retention and Deletion</a> for how long we keep information.

## Summary of Key Points

- We collect information you provide to create an account, sign in, build a profile, use the assistant, upload files, post content, or contact us.
- We also collect device, log, search, product interaction, and analytics data to operate, secure, and improve the Services.
- If you use the assistant, prompts, selected paper text, attachments, and related metadata may be processed by us and by third-party AI providers that help deliver the feature.
- We may share information with service providers that help us run the Services, including Google for optional Google sign-in, PostHog for analytics, Resend for email delivery, cloud hosting and storage providers, and AI providers or model routing providers used by the assistant.
- You can <a href="#delete-your-account" class="text-custom-red hover:underline">delete your account</a> in the app, on the web, or by email, delete assistant chats where available, or contact us to request access, correction, or deletion.
- We do not sell your personal information, provide it to data brokers, or use it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

## 1. Scope

This notice applies to alphaXiv's website, mobile apps, AI assistant features, and other services that link to this notice.

This notice does not apply to third-party websites, apps, or services that we do not control, even if you access them through our Services. Those third parties have their own terms and privacy practices.

## 2. Information We Collect

### Information you provide directly

Depending on how you use the Services, we may collect:

- **Account and profile information**, such as your name, email address, username, password credential or password hash, and profile details you choose to add.
- **Sign-in and linked account information**, such as your Google account identifier, email address, and basic profile information if you choose Google sign-in or link a Google account.
- **Content you create or submit**, such as comments, profile content, saved items, settings, and communications with us.
- **Assistant inputs and outputs**, such as prompts, messages, selected paper excerpts, chat history, and feedback on assistant responses.
- **Files and media you upload**, such as documents, images, photos, screenshots, or other attachments you submit to the assistant or other upload features.
- **Support and account communications**, such as emails you send us or information you provide when requesting help, changing your email, resetting a password, or deleting your account.

On mobile devices, we may request access to the camera, photo library, or document picker only when you choose to attach content or otherwise use a feature that needs that access.

### Information we collect automatically

When you use the Services, we may automatically collect:

- **Log and usage data**, such as IP address, browser type, device type, operating system, app version, timestamps, referrers, pages or screens viewed, searches, and feature usage.
- **Product interaction and analytics data**, such as events, interactions, navigation paths, search history, and engagement with specific features or content.
- **Device and app identifiers**, such as account-linked user IDs, application identifiers, and device identifiers used for security, fraud prevention, debugging, analytics, and product functionality.
- **Diagnostic and security data**, such as crash information, error reports, and rate-limiting or abuse-prevention signals.

### Information we receive from third parties

We may receive information from:

- **Identity providers**, such as Google, when you choose a third-party sign-in method.
- **Service providers**, such as analytics, email, hosting, storage, and AI vendors that help us operate the Services.
- **Other users or public sources**, if information about you is included in public content posted to the Services or provided in a paper, profile, comment, or other submission.

### Sensitive information

We do not ask for sensitive personal information as part of ordinary account creation or normal use of the Services.

However, because assistant prompts, comments, uploads, and other user content fields may be free-form, you may choose to submit information that is sensitive under some laws. If you do, we may process it to provide the feature you requested, secure the Services, investigate abuse, and comply with law. Do not submit sensitive or confidential information unless it is necessary for your use of the Services and you want it processed for that purpose.

## 3. How We Use Information

We may use personal information to:

- create, authenticate, secure, and manage accounts;
- provide the website, mobile apps, and related product features;
- deliver assistant, search, recommendation, and personalization features;
- process and store uploads, comments, chats, and other user content;
- remember settings such as language, theme, and account preferences;
- send transactional emails, verification codes, password reset emails, account notices, and service messages;
- analyze usage, debug issues, measure reliability, and improve the Services;
- detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, abuse, spam, security incidents, and violations of our terms;
- comply with legal obligations and enforce our agreements.

## 4. Assistant and AI Features

The assistant is an optional feature. When you use it, we may process:

- prompts and chat messages;
- selected paper text, citations, and related context;
- uploaded files, documents, photos, images, and screenshots;
- feedback about assistant responses;
- technical and usage metadata needed to run, secure, rate-limit, and improve the feature.

To provide assistant functionality, we may share this information with third-party AI and infrastructure providers that help us classify, filter, retrieve context, generate responses, route model requests, and improve reliability of the feature. These providers and model operators may include Google Gemini, Vertex AI, Cohere, OpenAI, Anthropic, OpenRouter, Tributary, Inception Labs, DeepSeek, Moonshot AI/Kimi, MiniMax, Qwen, and Z.ai/GLM. If you select a specific model or a custom routed model, the operator of that selected model may also receive the request content for that assistant request.

We do not authorize third-party AI providers to use assistant inputs, uploaded files, or outputs to train their general models. We use provider terms, product settings, contractual controls, or routing configurations intended to apply no-training and limited- or zero-retention handling for ordinary assistant requests. Provider-side transient processing, security logging, abuse monitoring, or legal retention may still occur depending on the selected provider, model, feature, and deployment unless a zero-retention configuration applies.

By choosing to submit prompts or attachments to the assistant, you direct us to process that content for the purpose of providing the assistant feature you requested.

We require service providers acting on our behalf to provide the same or equivalent protection for user data as described in this notice and as required by applicable App Store guidelines, and to use personal information only for providing services to us.

We do not sell personal information, provide it to data brokers, or use it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion

We keep personal information only for as long as needed for the purposes described in this notice, including to provide the Services, maintain security, resolve disputes, comply with law, and enforce our agreements.

In general:

- **Account and profile data** are retained while your account is active and for as long as needed to complete account administration, legal compliance, and security obligations.
- **Authentication, fraud-prevention, and security records** may be retained as long as reasonably necessary to secure the Services and investigate misuse.
- **Assistant chats** are retained until you delete the chat, delete your account, or we no longer need the data for the purposes described in this notice. Technical records of assistant requests, including prompts and responses, are kept after a chat is deleted, for debugging and operating the assistant, and are deleted when you delete your account.
- **Billing records** are held by our payment provider, Polar, and kept after account deletion as required for tax and accounting.
- **Public researcher profiles** built from publicly available information remain after account deletion, even if you claimed one, unless you ask us to change or remove them.
- **Assistant uploads and other files** are retained while needed to provide the feature, maintain storage lifecycle and backup processes, investigate abuse, resolve disputes, or comply with legal obligations.
- **Analytics and server logs** are retained for as long as reasonably necessary for operations, debugging, abuse detection, security, and aggregate reporting.
- **Public content** you choose to post may remain visible until you delete it, delete your account, or we remove it under our policies.

When you delete your account, we delete or deidentify personal information from active systems immediately, except for the records listed in <a href="#delete-your-account" class="text-custom-red hover:underline">Delete Your Account</a> and records we must keep for backup, security, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution, financial, tax, and other legal obligations. Deleting an account also ends access credentials and active sessions associated with that account.

Deletion may not immediately remove information from backups, cached systems, or storage replicas, but we do not keep those copies in routine use longer than reasonably necessary for the purposes described above.
