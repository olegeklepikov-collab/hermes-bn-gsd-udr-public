## Download Zotero Connector

**Zotero Connectors are for desktop only.** On mobile, you can easily save items and PDFs from the web to Zotero via the Share button in browsers and other apps.

- [Chrome](https://chrome.google.com/webstore/detail/ekhagklcjbdpajgpjgmbionohlpdbjgc)

- [Firefox](https://www.zotero.org/download/connector/dl?browser=firefox)

- [Edge](https://microsoftedge.microsoft.com/addons/detail/nmhdhpibnnopknkmonacoephklnflpho)

- The Zotero Connector for Safari is bundled with Zotero. You can enable it from the Extensions pane in the Safari settings.

  [Don’t see the Zotero Connector in Safari?](https://www.zotero.org/support/kb/safari_compatibility)
