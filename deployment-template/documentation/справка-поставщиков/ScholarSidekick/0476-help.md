What can I paste? DOI (e.g. 10.1038/nphys1170 or doi.org URL). Includes a “healer” for minor typos. PMID / PMCID (PMCID auto-maps to PMID/DOI if available). ISBN (10/13; dashes/spaces optional). Checking citations Beyond formatting, Scholar Sidekick can check whether a citation actually matches the record its identifier resolves to. These integrity tools live at Citation Verifier , Bibliography Audit , Retraction Checker , and Open-Access Checker . Citation Verifier checks a claimed citation against the record its identifier actually resolves to. It returns one of matched , mismatch , not_found , or ambiguous — and catches fabrications like a real DOI paired with an invented title. Bibliography Audit runs that same check plus retraction status across a whole pasted or uploaded reference list (BibTeX, RIS, or CSL-JSON). Verdicts are free on screen; an optional downloadable evidence report costs US$9. Retraction Checker and Open-Access Checker report whether a work has been retracted and whether it is freely readable. The matching API endpoints — /api/verify , /api/audit , /api/retraction-check , and /api/oa-check — mirror these checks. See /docs for request shapes. The long-form explainer is at /citation-integrity . What it can’t tell you The verifier checks that the citation matches the registry record — it does not judge the paper’s quality, and it can’t always tell apart translated or transliterated titles, online-first versus print years, or preprint versus published versions. A single-word meaning flip in an otherwise-identical title can also slip past it. The full list of known gaps is at /citation-integrity/known-failures.md . Speed & caching Recent lookups are cached so repeats are fast. Very new records may be briefly cached as “not found.” If you suspect a fresh registration, retry after a short delay or append ?nocache=1 in the UI. Developer note: responses include X-Scholar-Cache with BYPASS or ENABLED . Rate limits If you send many requests quickly, you may see 429 Too Many Requests . Back off per the Retry-After header. For developers We return X-RateLimit-Limit , X-RateLimit-Remaining , X-RateLimit-Reset , and Retry-After . Buckets differ by route (format, export, stream, etc.). See /api/health for a snapshot. Errors Friendly 4xx for input issues (e.g., invalid style/format). Line-level messages for failed lookups. Server errors are rare; we track them and improve reliability. Privacy We don’t share your input with third parties beyond the resolvers needed to fulfil your request (e.g., Crossref, PubMed). Logs are minimised and scrubbed. See Privacy Policy .
