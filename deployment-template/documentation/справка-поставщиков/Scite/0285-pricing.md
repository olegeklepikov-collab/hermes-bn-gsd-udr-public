# Scite pricing and access

> Plans for individual researchers, research teams, universities, and organizations using Scite's research platform, MCP server, API, and data.

Last updated: August 25, 2026. Prices are in US dollars. The interactive pricing page and checkout show the final price for a purchase.

## Try Scite MCP without an account

An AI agent can make up to 10 requests through the Scite MCP without creating a Scite account. No signup, credit card, or trial is required. After the free requests are used, sign in or create a free Scite account to receive 25 MCP credits per month.

- [Set up Scite MCP](https://scite.ai/mcp): Connect ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot, or another MCP-compatible client.
- [Remote MCP server](https://api.scite.ai/mcp): Streamable HTTP endpoint for MCP clients.
- [MCP discovery](https://scite.ai/.well-known/mcp.json): Machine-readable connection and authentication metadata.

## Plans at a glance

| Plan | Best for | Monthly billing | Annual billing | MCP allowance | API access |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Free | Trying Scite MCP | Free | Free | 25 credits/month | Not included |
| Basic | Individual researchers | $20/month | $144/year ($12/month) | 250 credits/month | Not included |
| Pro | Deep and advanced research | $50/month | $480/year ($40/month) | 2,500 credits/month | Included |
| Team | Labs and small teams | $50/seat/month | $480/seat/year ($40/seat/month) | 2,500 credits/month/user | Included for each user |
| Enterprise | Universities and organizations | Custom | Custom | Flexible pooled usage | Included |

Basic and Pro are available with monthly or annual billing. New individual subscribers may be eligible for a seven-day trial. Team subscriptions are paid plans and can include up to 15 seats through self-service checkout. Enterprise pricing is based on organizational requirements.

## Free

Free Scite accounts include 25 MCP credits per month. No paid subscription is required.

## Basic

Basic is for individual researchers who want Scite's core research workflows.

- Unlimited Assistant queries
- Full-text literature search
- Smart Citation Reports
- Citation alerts
- Claude and ChatGPT connectors
- 250 MCP credits per month
- Collections containing up to 1,000 papers
- $20 with monthly billing
- $144 with annual billing, equivalent to $12 per month

## Pro

Pro is for researchers who need higher limits and additional evidence sources.

- Everything in Basic
- 2,500 MCP credits per month
- Collections containing up to 10,000 papers
- Patents, clinical trials, and grants
- API access
- $50 with monthly billing
- $480 with annual billing, equivalent to $40 per month

## Team

Team provides Pro access to a lab or small team under one managed subscription.

- Everything in Pro
- 2,500 MCP credits per month for each user
- Shared Collections and alerts
- User analytics and reporting
- Self-service management for up to 15 seats
- $50 per seat with monthly billing
- $480 per seat with annual billing, equivalent to $40 per seat per month

## Academic access

Students and academic researchers can request a discount by recommending Scite to their institution. Email or forward the recommendation to both [customersupport@researchsolutions.com](mailto:customersupport@researchsolutions.com) and [sales@scite.ai](mailto:sales@scite.ai) to request a discount code.

Universities and libraries can purchase organization-wide access through an Enterprise agreement. Pricing depends on the number of faculty, students, and staff and may include volume-based discounts.

- [Scite for universities and libraries](https://scite.ai/partners/academic-institutions): Institutional use cases and access information.
- [Contact sales](https://scite.ai/contact-sales): Request a quote or demonstration for an institution.

## Enterprise

Enterprise is for universities, libraries, pharmaceutical companies, publishers, and other organizations that need broad access, administration, data, or integration support.

- Everything in Team
- Unlimited users
- Flexible pooled usage
- API access
- Regulatory and safety datasets, including FAERS, MAUDE, FDA 510(k), and MHRA data
- Extended usage reports
- Flexible access by domain, IP address, or email
- SAML single sign-on
- Dedicated customer success manager
- Enhanced security and data confidentiality
- Custom pricing through Scite sales

## API and data access

Pro includes API access. Scite also offers evidence datasets, bulk data, higher-volume API access, and custom licensing. Availability and usage limits depend on the plan and agreement.

- [API overview](https://scite.ai/api): Available API capabilities and data feeds.
- [API documentation](https://api.scite.ai/docs): Authentication, guides, examples, and endpoint reference.
- [Scite data](https://scite.ai/data): Scholarly, patent, clinical, grant, regulatory, and safety datasets.
- [Data coverage](https://scite.ai/coverage): Coverage statistics, subjects, and publisher partners.
- [Contact sales](https://scite.ai/contact-sales): Discuss API volume, data licensing, enterprise access, or custom requirements.

## Billing and support

- Individual plans can be canceled before a trial ends to avoid the first subscription charge.
- Existing subscribers can manage or change their plan from their Scite subscription page.
- Discounts and promotions may change the checkout price.
- [Interactive pricing page](https://scite.ai/pricing): View the current purchase interface and begin checkout.
- [Help and FAQ](https://help.researchsolutions.com/hc/en-us/categories/31947397460116-Scite): Get help with plans, accounts, and product access.
- [Subscription support](mailto:customersupport@researchsolutions.com): Ask about an existing account or subscription.
